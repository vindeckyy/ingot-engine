ingot-engine expanded logo asset pack

Contents
--------
master/       Original full-resolution logo compositions.
transparent/  Full logos with the white background converted to alpha.
dark/         Full logos composited onto a dark graphite background.
icon/         Emblem-only crops plus square app-icon sizes.
wordmark/     Wordmark-only crops.
web/          JPG and WebP exports for websites/docs.
favicons/     PNG favicon sizes and a multi-resolution .ico file.
social/       1200x630 light and dark social/GitHub preview cards.
reference/    Approximate color palette.

Notes
-----
The source artwork is raster/3D-rendered, so this pack intentionally does not include a fake SVG. A proper SVG should be manually redrawn as vectors rather than wrapping the PNG inside an SVG container.

The transparent versions are derived from the clean white source background and retain anti-aliased edges.
